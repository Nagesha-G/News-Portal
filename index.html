Global AI Summit 2025 Announced in New Delhi

October 15, 2025 | By Aarav Sharma

Leaders from over 50 countries are gathering to discuss the ethical future of Artificial Intelligence and quantum computing standards.

The summit aims to establish unified global policies for AI safety, open-source technology sharing, and digital infrastructure expansion across developing economies.

"Innovation must be accessible, safe, and beneficial to every human being regardless of geography."

— Dr. Sarah Jenkins

Special workshops will also focus on regional language technology development, ensuring non-English speaking populations benefit from machine learning tools.
